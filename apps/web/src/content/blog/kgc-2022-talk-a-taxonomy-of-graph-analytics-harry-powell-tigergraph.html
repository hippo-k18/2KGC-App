Please enjoy this talk “A Taxonomy of Graph Analytics,” from the Knowledge Graph Conference 2022 given by Harry Powell, Head of Industry Solutions at TigerGraph as part of KGC’s graph data science series.

Talk description:

In his presentation, “A Taxonomy Of Graph Analytics,” Powell shares a taxonomy of graph data analytics that reduces all graph analyses to four pairs of abstractions. He demonstrates how this makes it easier to solve graph analytics problems, using typical business challenges from several industries linked to real use cases.

Five quick takeaways from the presentation:

  1. What’s so “weird and rubbish” about conventional analytics?
  2. What’s the “subgraph equivalence?”
  3. The taxonomy of “nodes” and “edges.”
  4. The importance of “relatives,” “pattern matching” and “equivalence.”
  5. Outlining business use case examples in manufacturing, financial services, retail, and telecom.

About the speaker:

Harry Powell is the Head of Industry Solutions at TigerGraph, and was the Director of Data and Analytics at Jaguar Land Rover (JLR), as well as being the former Director, Head of Advanced Data Analytics at Barclays.

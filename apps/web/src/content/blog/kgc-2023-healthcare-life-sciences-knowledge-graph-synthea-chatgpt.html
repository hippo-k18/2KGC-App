Please enjoy this talk “Building a Healthcare & Life Sciences Knowledge Graph with Synthea & ChatGPT,” from the Knowledge Graph Conference 2023 given by Dr. Jans Aasman, CEO of Franz Inc.

Talk description:

Jans Aasman introduces a plug-and-play healthcare knowledge graph for hospitals, showcasing how large language models like ChatGPT can automatically answer questions and generate knowledge graphs.

The presentation also explores healthcare connected data and entity-event knowledge graphs, as well as GPT’s application in generating medical notes.

Jans demonstrates the collaboration between human expertise, large language models (LLMs), and knowledge graphs for future intelligent applications. He also highlights the challenges of ensuring accuracy in generated data.

Technical topics covered: 

  • Graph Databases
  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Knowledge Graph Technology
  • Knowledge Graph Applications

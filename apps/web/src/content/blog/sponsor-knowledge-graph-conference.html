What’s on the Agenda

The Knowledge Graph Conference 2025 promises a vibrant lineup. Here’s a brief look at what’s in store for this year’s agenda:

  • Masterclasses & Workshops: Hands-on learning for emerging technologies like GraphRAG and data orchestration
  • Healthcare & Life Sciences Symposium: A deep dive into how knowledge graphs are transforming these critical sectors

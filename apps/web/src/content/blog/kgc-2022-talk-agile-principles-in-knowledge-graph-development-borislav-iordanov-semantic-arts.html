Please enjoy this presentation “Agile Principles in Knowledge Graph Development” from the Knowledge Graph Conference 2022 given by Borislav Iordanov at Semantic Arts. 

Talk summary:

Part of the KGC 2022 “Ontologies & Taxonomies” series is “Agile Principles in Knowledge Graph Development” delivered by Borislav Iordanov of Semantic Arts.

The ontologist covers the application of agile principles in knowledge graph development. Iordanov delves into the key differences and similarities between the software development lifecycle and building of an enterprise knowledge graph — as well as its translation to concrete agile practices.

The KGC’s five key takeaways:

  1. The challenges of knowledge graphs.
  2. How are knowledge graphs different from software development?
  3. What is “parti”?
  4. What is the “Agile Manifesto”?
  5. The importance of the correct ETL process.

What’s going on about Knowledge Graphs

Google rolled out Watchlist in Search – The AI giant’s newest addition to its Knowledge Graph cards. Now users will be able to create watchlists right after searching for a film or a TV show.

OntoText Platform 3.0 released – OntoText’s latest effort at enterprise knowledge graphs, featuring significant technology improvements to enable simpler and faster graph navigation. Catch a glimpse of OntoText through their talk at KGC 2019.

Open Research Knowledge Graph Beta – A new initiative on the next generation digital libraries for semantic scientific knowledge communicated in scholarly literature, led by Sören Auer, one of the speakers at KGC 2019 where he shared the vision.

Open Graph Benchmark (OGB) – A large scale, high quality, and diverse set of benchmark datasets for machine learning on graphs, coming out recently from Stanford University.

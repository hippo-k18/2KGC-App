Please enjoy this presentation “A Workshop on Throwing Workshops” from the Knowledge Graph Conference 2024 given by Veronika Heimsbakk, Knowledge Graph Lead at Capgemini. 

Workshop description:

Welcome to an inspirational and creative meta-workshop on how to throw workshops! We will go hands-on, experience and learn first-hand how we can build successful workshops on outreaching the wonders of knowledge graphs.

The speaker has been fine tuning her findings through hosting these workshops for over three years, and will bring you onto her journey of knowledge exchange. The typical audience for such a workshop (outside KGC) would be business leaders, newcomers to graph technology and aspiring practitioners of knowledge graphs. At the Knowledge Graph Conference, you will get all the tips and tricks on how to throw an interactive and analogue knowledge graph workshop for your peers and/or clients, and be able to fine-tune the material even further together with fellow knowledge graph nerds.

No prerequisites needed. All tools of trade will be provided.

Technical topics: 

  • Semantic Web Technologies
  • Data Modeling
  • Knowledge Representation
  • Knowledge Graph Visualization

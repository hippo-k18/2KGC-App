Please enjoy this talk “Beyond Homophily in Graph Neural Networks (GNNs),” from the Knowledge Graph Conference 2024 given by Danai Koutra, Professor at the University of Michigan. 

Talk description:

In this talk, the University of Michigan’s Professor Danai Koutra presents her group’s research on identifying effective graph neural network (GNN) designs and introduces a new architectures that improves performance in heterophilous networks. Professor Koutra also discusses connections between heterophily and other well-known challenges of GNNs, including oversmoothing and robustness.

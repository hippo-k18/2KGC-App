The health and wellbeing of our community is our top priority. Due to the growing concern around the coronavirus (COVID-19), and in alignment with the best practices laid out by the CDC, WHO and other relevant entities, The Knowledge Graph Conference has decided to reimagine KGC 2020, which will still take place from May 4th to 7th.

We are transforming the event into a global, digital-first, multi-day event connecting our attendees to our speakers and each other through streamed keynotes, breakout sessions, workshops, tutorials and more. 

This opens up lots of potential for the event and while we lose some of the in-person dynamics, we expect to craft a unique experience and also engage a much broader global audience as a result.

As we work through the details of this new digital experience over the coming weeks, we will continue to share updates with you via our newsletter.

Stay safe and we look forward to seeing you all online in May.

Cordially,
The KGC team.

Please enjoy this panel discussion “Knowledge Graph Architecture: Where Are We & Where Are We Going?” from the Knowledge Graph Conference 2022 shared by Sara Nash, Juan Sequeda, and Gregor Wobbe. 

Panel discussion summary:

The panelists discussed the following topics:

  • The state of knowledge graph architecture today
  • Where should we be going?
  • Lessons learned and the mistakes made
  • The relationships with the rest of the modern data stack
  • The importance of company recruitment and upskilling

About the participants:

Moderator:

  • Juan Sequeda: the Principal Scientist at data.world. Mr. Sequeda is a recipient of the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship and holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Texas at Austin.

Guest speakers:

  • Gregor Wobbe: the Head of Data Architecture and Executive Director for UBS. Before joining UBS, Mr. Wobbe held senior positions at JPMorgan Chase and Bank of America.
  • Sara Nash: a Senior Consultant at Enterprise Knowledge. Ms. Nash has professional experience with knowledge management consulting, web startups, and institutional research in higher education

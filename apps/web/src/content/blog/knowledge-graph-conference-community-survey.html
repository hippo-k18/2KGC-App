Thank you for making the Knowledge Graph Conference 2025 the best edition ever! 

Your feedback on the content and experience of KGC is what makes each year better than the last. This summer, we want to take a pulse on some of your ideas for KGC 2026 as we brainstorm about expanding the scope of the content for next year’s conference.

If you attended KGC 2025 – or if you plan to attend KGC 2026 – please take 3-4 minutes to fill out the KGC Community Survey with your thoughts and ideas on how we can improve the conference for next year and what sort of content you’re most interested in learning more about. We’ll share the survey results with all of the survey respondents.

The conference typically includes or covers as wide ranging as:

  • Knowledge graphs
  • AI
  • Ontologies
  • Graph data science
  • LLMs
  • Taxonomies
  • Graph neural networks
  • Semantic technology
  • Graph databases
  • Related topics

But are there other topics, technologies, or areas you’d like to see represented at the Knowledge Graph Conference in the future? Let us know!

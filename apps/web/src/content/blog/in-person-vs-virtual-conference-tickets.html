In the meantime, do you know what kind of ticket you want to buy? There’s important benefits and trade-offs to both the All-Access (In-Person) ticket and the Virtual conference ticket. 

Here’s a quick breakdown of what each ticket type includes, so you can choose the best experience for you.

Virtual Ticket

The Virtual ticket option is the perfect option for introverted, budget-conscious, or on-the-go attendees.

If you prefer to attend from the comfort of your own home, office, or home office – pajamas optional, but encouraged – yet you still want to have full access to the core content of the Knowledge Graph Conference, then the Virtual ticket is right for you.

Here’s what’s included with your Virtual ticket:

  • Access to all virtual conference sessions: Join live talks, keynotes, and panels directly from your phone or laptop. 
  • The KGC event app: Unlock virtual networking opportunities via the official KGC event app with access to attendee profiles, virtual sponsor booths, community boards, and the ability to chat directly with other participants. Access to the app opens in February so you have plenty of time to network and meet fellow attendees!
  • Personalized conference agenda: Browse the full agenda of speaking sessions and then create your own schedule by selecting the ones you don’t want to miss and the event app will remind you automatically of what’s next.

All-Access (In-Person) Ticket

Looking for a deeper, more hands-on experience at Knowledge Graph Conference 2025?

The All-Access ticket includes everything from the Virtual ticket, plus the opportunity to rub shoulders and spend time with the world’s leading experts in an intimate setting on the beautiful campus at Cornell Tech in the heart of NYC. There’s a limited supply of All-Access tickets, so if you’re looking for a richer and more unique experience at the Knowledge Graph Conference, then be sure to get your ticket today.

Here’s what you get with an All-Access ticket:

  • In-person & virtual sessions: Enjoy full access to both in-person sessions and virtual content throughout the entire conference (Monday-Friday).
  • Workshops & tutorials: Participate in exclusive hands-on workshops and tutorials led by knowledge graph and AI industry experts.
  • Networking events: Attend in-person networking events to build connections with thought leaders, peers, colleagues, and sponsors in a more personal setting.
  • Access to all session recordings: Gain access to all virtual session recordings, so you can revisit your favorite moments or catch up on anything you missed.

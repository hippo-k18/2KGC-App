Please enjoy this talk “Composite Queries for Multidomain Knowledge: Span Multiple Graphs without Blending Data,” from the Knowledge Graph Conference 2024 given by David Hughes,  Director of Engineering & AI at Graphable (now at Enterprise Knowledge). 

Talk description:

In the rapidly evolving landscape of data management, the ability to effectively handle diverse concerns such as data privacy, sensitivity, and scalability is paramount. This talk outlines a novel architecture that employs a graph query engine to manage these concerns, alongside the use of multiple databases and composite queries for comprehensive graph analysis. The session showcases how this architecture facilitates rapid prototyping and analysis across varying schema on-read models, and how it integrates enriching databases and ontologies for a more robust knowledge graph.  

Technical topics covered: 

  • Data Modeling
  • Knowledge Representation
  • Querying Knowledge Graphs
  • Graph Databases & Storage
  • Knowledge Graph Visualization

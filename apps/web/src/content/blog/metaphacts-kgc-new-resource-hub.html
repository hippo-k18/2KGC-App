The knowledge graph landscape and community have transformed dramatically over the years, shaping the way we understand, model, share and interact with knowledge. As the adoption of artificial intelligence continues to redefine the boundaries of what’s possible, the role of knowledge graphs as a foundational layer for trustworthy and explainable AI-driven solutions has grown more critical than ever. 

Recognizing the need to support the evolving community, metaphacts and the Knowledge Graph Conference (KGC) are thrilled to announce the launch of the KGC Resource Hub – a knowledge graph-powered app designed to empower users to delve into the rich archives of past KGC content while offering insights into the future.

This new tool is not just a response to the community’s growth; it’s a step toward ensuring that the collective wisdom of KGC participants are recorded and organized in a way that fuels ongoing innovation and collaboration in the knowledge graph space.

A Resource for the Thriving Knowledge Graph Community

The KGC Resource Hub is more than a digital archive. It’s a curated gateway for the growing KGC community.

Whether you’re a newcomer or a seasoned expert, the app makes it easier than ever for users to explore relevant content tailored to their interests, discover trending topics, and find experts for particular subjects or like-minded professionals solving similar challenges.

By unlocking years of presentations, papers, tutorials, masterclasses, workshops, roundtables and expert content, the Resource Hub offers invaluable opportunities for learning and collaboration.

“Our goal with the KGC Resource Hub is to provide the knowledge graph community with a tool that not only looks back but also lays the foundation for future innovation,” said François Scharffe, Co-Founder of the Knowledge Graph Conference. “Our community and the graph technology landscape are constantly evolving, and this app ensures that the ideas of the past are always accessible to inspire what’s next.”

The KGC Resource Hub is also a window into upcoming KGC events. Alongside its extensive archive of past content, the app features the program and highlights of each upcoming conference, including keynotes, industry sessions, tutorials, topics and emerging trends that are set to take center stage.

Users can explore planned presentations, discover speakers shaping the future of the field, and find opportunities to engage directly with thought leaders. By connecting past learnings with future possibilities, the Resource Hub reinforces KGC’s mission to foster a forward-thinking, collaborative community.

Powered by metaphactory

The KGC Resource Hub is based on the metaphacts Resource Hub and was built using metaphactory, leveraging the platform’s full potential – from semantic knowledge modeling to app building. From knowledge modeling and curation to the sleek user-friendly interface, the entire app was crafted using the capabilities of metaphactory.

The underlying semantic model that powers the KGC Resource Hub is based on schema.org, ensuring structured, interoperable metadata that enhances usability and accessibility, as well as discoverability by search engines. 

“This project exemplifies what metaphactory was designed for: enabling powerful, semantic-driven solutions,” explained Thomas Kaminski, Pre-sales Engineer at metaphacts and the technical lead on the project. “From start to finish, it stands as a testament to how the platform ultimately supports end users in extracting meaningful insights from data.”

A Word from the KGC Community

The launch of the KGC Resource Hub was made possible through the tight collaboration between the Knowledge Graph Conference and metaphacts, a strong supporter of the knowledge graph community. Scharffe, a key figure in the KGC ecosystem, praised the collaboration that brought the app to life:

“The KGC community thrives on collaboration and shared knowledge,” said Scharffe. “We deeply appreciate partners like metaphacts who share our vision and create tools like the KGC Resource Hub that help share their learnings and empower discovery & innovation. It’s exciting to see such commitment to fostering connections and unlocking new possibilities.”

Discover the KGC Resource Hub

The KGC Resource Hub is now available for the KGC community (and beyond) from the KGC website. Featuring a clean, intuitive interface and enriched with years of conference content, it’s designed to help users uncover ideas and connections that might otherwise remain hidden and explore what future events have in store.

To celebrate the launch, the metaphacts and KGC teams will be publishing a recorded demo of the KGC Resource Hub in early 2025. The demo will provide insights into how end-users can navigate the app and find content they are interested in, as well as technical details on the app’s backend knowledge graph. 

Take your first step toward unlocking the future of knowledge graphs: Explore the KGC Resource Hub today and start learning.

Features and steps

Content

Edit event basics

Works

Sets the event name, short name, start and end dates, time zone, venue and event type. The website header and footer, the agenda day tabs and the app home screen all read them.

  1. Open Content > Basics.
  2. Change the name, short name, dates, time zone, venue or event type.
  3. Click Save.
  4. Check the website header and the app home screen show the new details.

/content/basics

Edit website copy (three pages)

Partial

Edits the code of conduct reporting contact, and the submission link, button label and deadlines on the call for posters and startup pitch pages.

  1. Open Content > Basics.
  2. Click Website Copy.
  3. Edit the reporting address and committee. Click Save reporting route.
  4. Edit a call's submission URL, button label and deadlines. Click Save this call.

Only these fields on three pages. All other website text is fixed in code. Plain text only.

/content/basics/website-copy

Set tagline, support email and brand colours

Partial

Saves the tagline, support address, hashtag and two brand colours.

  1. Open Content > Branding Center > App Branding.
  2. Enter tagline, support address, hashtag and colours.
  3. Click Save.

Tagline and support email reach the website. Colours and hashtag are saved but nothing reads them.

Works

  1. Open Content > Branding Center > Branded Event URL.
  2. Enter the slug.
  3. Click Save.

/content/branding-center/branded-event-url

Customize app tabs and resources

View only

Shows the five fixed app tabs, and holds the custom pages for the app and the website.

  1. Open Content > Branding Center > Customize Resources.

The five app tabs are fixed and cannot be added to, reordered or renamed. Custom pages written here do reach the app and the website.

/content/branding-center/customize-resources

Write a custom page for the app and website

Partial

Writes pages such as Wi-Fi, getting here or an FAQ, with headings, lists, links, bold and code. A published page appears at its own address on the website and in the app’s home section.

  1. Open Content > Branding Center > Customize Resources.
  2. Click New page.
  3. Enter the title, the summary and the web address.
  4. Write the body.
  5. Set it to published, or leave it as a draft.
  6. Click Save.
  7. Open that address on the website to check it.

Pages save, publish and render on the test event. Waiting on the new access settings being published to the live event.

Add a session

Works

Creates a session with time, room, tracks, speakers, format, capacity and status.

  1. Open Content > Agenda Center > Session Manager.
  2. Pick a day tab.
  3. Click Add session.
  4. Enter title, start, end, location, tracks and speakers.
  5. Set Status to published.
  6. Click Create session.

/content/agenda-center/session-manager

Edit a session

Works

Changes any field on an existing session, including cancelling it.

  1. Open Content > Agenda Center > Session Manager.
  2. Pick the day tab.
  3. Click Edit on the session.
  4. Change the fields.
  5. Click Save.

/content/agenda-center/session-manager

Tell attendees when a session moves

Blocked

Writes a notice to everybody who has the session in their schedule when you change its room or its time, and shows it to them in the app.

  1. Open Content > Agenda Center > Session Manager.
  2. Click Edit on the session.
  3. Change the room or the time.
  4. Click Save.

The notice is written by a background job that is not switched on yet, so the room changes and nobody is told. Announce it yourself for now.
